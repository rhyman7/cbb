Click a column to sort. Stats count games between two Division I teams; W-L counts every game. The number before a team is its AP Top 25 rank. Adj. off and Adj. def are points scored and allowed per 100 possessions, adjusted for the opponents each team has faced and for home court (a ridge regression over every game, the same method as the football EPA ratings). Adj. net is the difference. Net is the raw version. SRS is the same idea in points per game: how many points better than an average team on a neutral floor. SoS is the strength of schedule in points. The last eight columns are the Four Factors on offense and defense.

Stats cover men's Division I games against other Division I teams this season, through the latest finished games, from sportsdataverse's hoopR data. Records count every game. Ranks are out of all Division I teams; the number before a team is its AP Top 25 rank. Live scores and lines: ESPN.
